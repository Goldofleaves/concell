This game supports both mouse control and keyboard control.

Mouse control: Click and drag to move, left click to select/use item and progress cutscenes, right click to discard/cancel items.
Keyboard control: F5 to fullscreen, arrow keys to plan move, numbers 1 - 4 to select/use item from its corresponding item slot, space/enter key to conifirm moving and C to cancel movement, F1 - 4 to discard/cancel item from its corresponding item slot
